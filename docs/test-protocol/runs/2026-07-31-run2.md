## New finding

### KI-1 — Stale left-panel child-count badge (papercut, deferred)
Surfaced by 12.8's delete-then-`h` scenario: the left column's child-count
badge for the fixture dir lags the on-disk count by one. Distinct from and
unrelated to the (fixed) preview bug — center listing and preview are correct.
Root cause is the mtime-gated panel cache being unable to observe a grandchild
change; a fix would trade away a deliberate performance optimization. Full
analysis and the maintainer's fix option are in
[KNOWN-ISSUES.md](../KNOWN-ISSUES.md). Step 12.8 now explicitly tells executors
not to assert the left-panel badge.
